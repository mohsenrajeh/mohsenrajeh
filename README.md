### A bit about me

I'm a Shiraz-born builder with nearly two decades of hands-on work across WordPress,
WooCommerce, SEO, automation, trading tools, and full product systems. These days I lead
[Agzon](https://agzon.ir) and spend most of my time turning messy real-world operations
into software people can actually use.

I like products with a pulse: live data, clear states, useful automation, and enough polish
that the technology disappears into the job.

### What I'm usually building

- AI-assisted product and content workflows
- WooCommerce tooling for real store operations
- Dashboards that turn operational data into decisions
- Browser extensions, crawlers, and internal automation
- Real-time audio, media, and creator tools
